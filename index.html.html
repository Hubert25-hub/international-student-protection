At the same time, federal proposals to cut study-permit numbers by more than half within three years are being used to justify shrinking the very campus supports — advising, housing help, mental health services — that international students rely on most. The pressure is landing on the students who have the least power to push back.

WHAT WE'RE DEMANDING

Three protections the petition calls for

01 · HOUSING

Enforced safety & occupancy standards

Real inspection and enforcement against landlords who overcrowd and exploit student tenants, with a reporting channel that doesn't put a student's status at risk.

02 · WORK RULES

Stable, predictable work-hour policy

An end to abrupt, unannounced changes to off-campus work limits — students need enough notice to plan rent, tuition, and course loads around the rules.

03 · CAMPUS SUPPORT

Protected funding for student services

Guaranteed baseline funding for advising, housing help, and mental health services for international students, independent of year-to-year enrolment swings.
